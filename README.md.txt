
Ferns and Petals (FNP) Sales Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview:

This project analyzes sales data from Ferns and Petals (FNP), an online gifting platform, to uncover key business insights related to sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, and delivery efficiency.
The analysis is performed using Excel (Dashboard + Data Analysis) and focuses on solving real-world business problems.

 Objectives:

To analyze sales data and answer key business questions that help improve;
Revenue generation
Customer satisfaction
Product strategy
Operational efficiency

 Project Structure:

Raw Data → Original dataset
Excel Working → Data cleaning & analysis
Dashboard → Interactive Excel dashboard
Output → Final insights & visuals

 Key Metrics:

 Total Revenue: ₹35,20,984
 Total Orders: 1000
 Avg Delivery Time: 5.53 days
 Avg Customer Spending: ₹3,520.98

 Key Insights:
 Sales Trends:-

Peak sales observed in February and August
Strong seasonality based on festivals and occasions

Occasion-Based Performance:-
Anniversary & Raksha Bandhan generate highest revenue
Events like Holi & Valentine’s Day also contribute significantly

Product Insights:-
Top categories: Colors, Soft Toys, Sweets
Best-performing products are gift bundles & premium items

 City-wise Orders:-
Highest orders from cities like Imphal, Dhanbad, Kavali
Indicates region-specific demand patterns

Order Time Behavior:-
Evening hours show highest order activity
Useful for targeted ads & offers

Delivery Performance:-
Average delivery time is stable at 5.53 days

Order Quantity vs Delivery Time:-
Correlation: 0.003478174
Indicates no relationship between order size and delivery time
Shows efficient and scalable logistics system

Dashboard Features:

KPI Cards (Revenue, Orders, Avg Spend, Delivery Time)
Revenue by Occasion
Monthly Sales Trend
Revenue by Category
Top 5 Products
Top 10 Cities by Orders
Revenue by Hour
Interactive Slicers (Date & Occasion)

 Tools Used:

Microsoft Excel
Pivot Tables
Charts
Slicers & Timelines
Data Cleaning

Business Recommendations:

Focus on high-revenue occasions
Improve sales in low-performing months
Promote top-selling products
Use evening-time marketing strategies
Expand in high-demand cities
Leverage upselling (bundles, combos)

 Conclusion:

The project highlights how seasonality, customer behavior, and product strategy drive sales.
It also shows that FNP has a strong delivery system, capable of handling different order sizes efficiently.
